Job Match SA

Overview

Job Match SA is a data engineering project that collects, processes, and analyzes job listings from multiple sources to help job seekers find opportunities that match their skills and experience.

The platform uses data engineering principles to ingest, clean, transform, and store job market data before generating recommendations and insights for users.

Problem Statement

Job seekers often search across multiple job platforms to find relevant opportunities. This process can be time consuming and may result in missed opportunities.

Job Match SA aims to centralize job data, provide skill-based job recommendations, and identify skill gaps that may affect employability.

Objectives

* Collect job data from multiple sources
* Clean and standardize job information
* Store processed data in a structured database
* Match candidate skills with job requirements
* Generate job market insights
* Identify skill gaps for career development
